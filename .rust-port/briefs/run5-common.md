# Common rules for fifth-session tracks (Phase 12 completion)

Read `.rust-port/briefs/run4-h12-common.md` first, and `.rust-port/briefs/run3-common.md`, which it names; everything in both applies (toolchain line, where you work, tests, logs to your staging dir, commits with the secret scan and `--no-verify`, the report), with these differences.

- The environment was rebuilt on a fresh host: Rust stable 1.100.0 and 1.96.0 under `/mnt/workspace/.rust`, Node under `/mnt/workspace/.node/bin`, the TS spec export at `/tmp/ts-spec` (strands-ts and harness-ts at the pin), the donor at `/tmp/donor-strandly`. `/tmp/ref-witcherish` does not exist (the repository is gone); ignore references to it.
- Your worktree starts with a cold target dir; the first `cargo check -p strands-harness --all-features --tests` compiles every dependency. Start it in the background right away (output to a file under `/tmp`) while you read.
- The whole harness crate is written and integrated (`harness-rs/strands-harness/src/`). The SDK crate `strands` is complete and reviewed; change it only where your brief allows, in the smallest `feat(strands): ...` commit of its own, with a pending decision.
- Tool output over about 6 KB is cut; read files in slices and send long command output to files under `/tmp`.
- You are cut off without warning about 100 minutes after you start. Commit after every step that compiles with its tests passing. Stop starting new work after about 80 minutes: `cargo fmt --all`, the per-crate gate steps listed in `run4-h12-common.md`, commit, write the staging files, report.
- Up to four tracks build at once on 4 cores. Keep `CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`. Never kill processes by name.
- The title checker must end with no FAIL for the specs you own: `NODE_PATH=/mnt/workspace/harness-sdk/node_modules node strands-rs/docs/check-test-titles.mjs --parity harness-rs/docs/PARITY.md` from your worktree root (it reads `harness-rs/docs/test-title-map.json`; columns are matched/mapped/declared gap/missing). In this session you DO edit `harness-rs/docs/test-title-map.json` (entries of your own specs only) and you still stage PARITY rows in `.rust-port/staging/<track>/parity.md` as full replacement rows. A case is closed by a ported test (the snake_case title, or a map entry naming the Rust test), or by a declared gap whose reason is true and specific (out of scope by a named decision, a TS-only mechanism that the Rust types cannot express, deprecated). "Not ported yet" is not a reason.
- No severity or priority labels, no time estimates, public names only, hyphens rather than em dashes in prose.
