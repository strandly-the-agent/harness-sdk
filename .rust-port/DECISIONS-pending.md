Options: raise the MSRV to 1.98, or keep 1.96 (#59) and let the MSRV-aware resolver 3 pick 1.5.0. Choice: keep 1.96; `monty = "1.5"` in `[workspace.dependencies]`, locked at 1.5.0. Moving to 1.6 needs an MSRV bump in a minor release (#59). TS reference: none. Files: `Cargo.toml`, `Cargo.lock`.

**P-6. `cargo deny` graph and license list.** Context: `monty` pulls `postcard`, whose `heapless` dependency uses `atomic-polyfill` (RUSTSEC-2023-0089, unmaintained) only on targets without native atomics; `cedar-policy` pulls `minicbor` (BlueOak-1.0.0) and `monty` pulls `unicode_names2` (Unicode-DFS-2016). Choice: `[graph] targets` lists the Linux (gnu and musl), macOS and Windows x86_64/aarch64 targets, which removes the embedded-only crate without an ignore; BlueOak-1.0.0 and Unicode-DFS-2016 (both permissive and Apache-2.0 compatible) join the allowlist. Duplicate versions warn generally and are denied for `reqwest`, `hyper`, `rustls`, `h2`, `tokio`, `serde_json`, `opentelemetry`, `opentelemetry_sdk` and `cedar-policy-core`. TS reference: none. Files: `deny.toml`.
