**P-266. Process spawning: groups, termination and decoding.** `stream_process` spawns through `process-wrap` with `ProcessGroup::leader()` (Unix) or `JobObject` (Windows) plus `KillOnDrop` (P-5, section 11 item 42b). On timeout or cancel it sends `SIGTERM` to the group (Windows: kill) and returns the error at once, as TS does; a spawned, instrumented reaper sends `SIGKILL` to the group after TS's one-second grace. Dropping the stream kills the group. An already-cancelled signal fails before spawning (TS spawns and kills at once; the result is the same error). stdin is always a pipe: written and closed when `input` is set, otherwise held open for the life of the stream (TS "keeps stdin open"). Output is decoded incrementally like Node's `StringDecoder` (split sequences held, invalid bytes become U+FFFD). Signal exits map to `128 + n` for TS's eight named signals and `128 + 1` otherwise. TS reference: `sandbox/stream-process.ts`. Files: `src/sandbox/stream_process.rs`.
