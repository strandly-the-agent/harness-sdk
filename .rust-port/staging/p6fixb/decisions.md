# p6fixb decisions (P-620..)

**P-620. TS-internal context manager items are crate-private; corrects P-310.** Review finding api-6: P-310 listed items as public that TS marks `@internal` and exports from neither `index.ts` nor `experimental/index.ts`. Choice: `pub(crate)` for `Stash::new` (old public `Stash::new(storage, session_id, agent_id, custom_namespace)`; a stash is only created by a `ContextManager`), `Stash::clear_session`, `Stash::take_snapshot`, `Stash::load_snapshot`, `EmergencyTruncateStrategy` (and its `NAME`, `new`), `BLOCK_SUMMARY_INSTRUCTIONS`, `DROPPED_MARKER`, `RETRIEVAL_TOOL_NAME` and `STRATEGY_PRESET_NAMES` (which also takes the `[StrategyPresetName; 4]` length out of the public API, finding api-7). `Stash` keeps what a custom `ContextStrategy` calls: `store`, `refs_for`, `store_message`, `retrieve`, `list`, `delete`, `clear`, `storage_type_name`, `is_durable`. P-310 also says the `strategies::offload::{base, drop, emergency, summarize, truncate}` modules are public; they are private and their public types are re-exported from `strategies::offload` and the `context_manager` root. `strands::experimental` now also re-exports `TruncateConfig`, `SummarizeConfig` and `PreviewMode` (TS `experimental/index.ts:17-18`; api-8). TS reference: `context-manager/stash.ts:68`, `strategies/offload/base.ts:544`, `summarize.ts:40`, `drop.ts:4`, `retrieval-tool.ts:6`. Files: `src/experimental.rs`, `src/experimental/context_manager.rs`, `src/experimental/context_manager/{stash,presets,retrieval_tool}.rs`, `src/experimental/context_manager/strategies/offload/{drop,emergency,summarize}.rs`, `src/experimental/context_manager/strategies/offload.rs`.

**P-621. Whole-history token counts go through `ModelProxy`; corrects P-317.** P-317 kept direct `model.count_tokens` calls because the Rust `ModelProxy` had no `count_tokens`; it has one now (`models/model_proxy.rs`). Choice: `ModelProxy::new(&*model).count_tokens(..)` at the three whole-history sites (TS `context-manager/context-manager.ts:212,232`, `strategies/offload/base.ts:560`), so summary and injected blocks are counted in the form the request sends; per-block counts stay direct as in TS (`base.ts:463,511`). The strategy loop sets `any_acted` before the recount (TS `context-manager.ts:229-233`). Files: `src/experimental/context_manager.rs`, `src/experimental/context_manager/strategies/offload/emergency.rs`.

**P-622. `StashOption` and `MemorySetting<T>` stay two shapes.** Review finding api-16. TS `stash?: StashConfig | boolean` treats `true` as the default config, so `StashOption::{Enabled(StashConfig), Disabled}` loses nothing and `From<bool>` maps `true` to `Enabled(StashConfig::default())`. `MemorySetting<T>::{Off, On, Config(T)}` (P-351) keeps `On` apart from `Config(T)` because its config types have no total default independent of the store. Choice: keep both; `ContextManagerConfig::stash` is now `#[builder(into)]` like the `MemoryManagerConfig` fields, so `.stash(false)` and `.stash(StashConfig { .. })` compile. `ContextStrategyEntry` gains `From<Arc<S>>` (api-11) and `ContextManagerPreset` gains `as_str` and `Display` (api-14). Files: `src/experimental/context_manager.rs`.
