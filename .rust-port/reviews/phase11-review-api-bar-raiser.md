## Verdict

Not ready to freeze as a first-release surface: nothing fails to build or document, but six cross-module rules (flat re-exports, `NAME`, public-dependency re-exports, `Copy`, shared-handle acceptance, builder nameability) are each applied in the phase that decided them and not in the others, and the two crates cannot be packaged as they stand (findings 4 and 5).
